Scope

This diagram covers only the userspace path, from cryptsetup luksOpen to the dm_create_device hand-off. The kernel side it hands to — the master key's lifecycle and per-sector tweak/IV — is in key-lifecycle.html and tweak-iv.html. The first phase here (prove the passphrase) is exactly what cryptsetup open --test-passphrase and scripts/recover-passphrase run.
